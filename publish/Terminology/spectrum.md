A spectrum is a term used to define something that goes through many different categories or to hit every number between two.

For example, a spectrum between the number 1 and 4 would contain every single number between, such as 2, 3.1415, etc. 

In other ways spectrum is used to define, for example, sexuality; whereas one person may like people of the opposite gender, another may have their own preferences. In this way a spectrum is defined.

Spectrums are also used for disorders and mental health conditions. For example, the autism spectrum is a popularly cited example. As one person has extreme autistic symptoms, another may have little, as all persons are built and develop differently and uniquely from one-another.
