Hard disk drives, also known as HDDs, are basically just spinning platters of magnetic material that you can write data upon.

They are known for their cost-efficiency, although face issues with reliability and speed.

Since they are magnetic, they are instantly destroyed if any dust or material get inside of them. Furthermore, they are bulky. However, a huge amount of data can be written onto them, and unlike [[Solid State Drive]]s, they do not lose data by the chips slowly getting worn out. They are reliable in the sense that they are usually used in long running applications, however they are not fast. 

They provide data security in the sense that they can be forensically erased by using erasure mechanisms and magnets to completely erase these drives. However, unlike an SSD, this does not impede forensic analysis and even the best erasure mechanisms are able to be circumvented and some data can be extracted.
