Also called ATIS, it is the broadcast of aviation information in busy terminal areas to aircraft. 
Each ATIS message is required to have a phonetic code word at the beginning and the end. Specifically, each must be used sequentially. 

The following is composited based on information of [Section 9, 2-9-2](https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/atc_html/chap2_section_9.html) of the 7110.65BB FAA JO Order of [[Air Traffic Control|ATC]] Operating Procedures 

ATIS must make reports that include the weather and runway breaking actions, alongside other items.
