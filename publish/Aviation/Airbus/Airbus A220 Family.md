The Airbus A220 is a family of narrow-body airliners that are made for shorter routes - typically domestic routes in the US and elsewhere. This family has **two** planes: the A220-100 and A220-300.

Both planes are similar; their cabin width, wingspan, height, engines, and etc are the same; alognisde their fuel capacity. The 100 series has a max range of 3600 nautical miles, whilst the 300 series has a max range of 3400 nautical miles.

Both planes have only two engines - both are geared turbofan engines
