Oh no! I'm working on a 10 foot high building (I have a deathly fear of heights). How can I protect myself? Simple. Use fall protection, which come in two types: arresting & restraining. 

## Fall Arrest
Fall arresting systems will help in case of a fall. These are clipped into a secure structure, such as being wrapped around a bar, a strong anchor point, or another permanent or portable anchoring point. If you fall, this point will be there to catch the harness. 

However, this method has many more considerations than a Fall Restraint system.
### Considerations
The height matters when making a Fall Arrest plan. For small falls, a simple stretching lanyard could be suitable. However, for higher falls, you must consider the amount of force the person will feel when they come to a stop. Many Fall Arrest systems use ropes with a packaged fabric spring-like system, which, in a fall, will activate and allow the worker to come to a stop over a longer period of time. 

## Fall Restraint
**Fall Restraints have you hooked up to a cable or other restraint system, which makes you unable to fall. For example, you might be locked into the center of a platform, so you can work on the edges but not over them: a leash.**

These restraints can take the form of the leaching system, cables along work surface, and other areas. Another restraint method that is less active is simply having the falling areas fenced off through guardrails, which allow workers to pass by with little risk of falling.
