Linux is an open-source operating system that is free to download and distribute with no restrictions under the GNU license. Linux is packaged as a distro which include the internal [[system kernel]] and all necessary libraries, alongside different tools depending on the operating system. Some popular distributions of Linux include Ubuntu, Arch, Mint, Fedora, Debian, and others. Some commercial variations include the red hat series, which is often used in cybersecurity work. 

Linux is also the primary operating system for servers, including supercomputers, due to its customizability. Linux has open sourcing and this results in massive amounts of documentation for the most popular distributions. This customizability means you can even install it completely by hand for maximum customizability and control over your own system. Linux can be ran on a USB drive, CD-ROM, and other sources.
