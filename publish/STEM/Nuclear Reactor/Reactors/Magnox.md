Magnox is a type of [[Nuclear Reactor]] designed in the UK. It was designed to run on [[Uranium]]. The moderator is graphite (Which reminds me of a certain other [[RBMK Reactor]]), and uses CO2 as the heat-exchanger coolant, being part of the wider gas-cooled reactors. 

Magnox stands for MAGnesium Non-OXidizing -- from the magnesium-aluminnum alloy used to in [[reactor cladding]].

Presently, the only country to still use Magnox reactors are North Korea.
