Uranium is a naturally occuring element - atomic number of 92 and in the actinides group. Uranium is radioactive - decaying over time and releasing energy. This is the reason why Uranium is the primary source of fuel for [[Nuclear Reactor]]s. 

The amount of energy in a *single gram* of uranium is about 3.5-4.5 tons of coal; around 702 gigajoules - whilst each gram of coal has around .0293 gigajoles of energy; a staggering amount inside of uranium. 

## Isotopes
There are three **natural** isotopes of uranium - 234, 235, and 238. Most reactors use U-235, using fuel enrichment methods. Only [[CANDU Reactor]]s can use unenriched uranium.
