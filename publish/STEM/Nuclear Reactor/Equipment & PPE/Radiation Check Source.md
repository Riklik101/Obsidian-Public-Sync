A check source is a small sticker containing a small amount of a varying radioisotope that decays and releases the specified Alpha, Beta, Gamma, or Electron decay. This is used on manual radiation detectors to check if they are working

These radiation check-sources usually come with +- 20% variation, where if the detector exceeds it it needs to be recallibrated and sent for maintenance.
Otherwise, some companies are able to calibrate the check sources to have +- 5% variation
