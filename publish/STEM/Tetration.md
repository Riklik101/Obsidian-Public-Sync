TL:DR Tetration is basically exponentiation, but simplified.

See: exponentiation is multiplication:
$4^2 = 4*4 = 16$  
Therefore, exponentiation is proven. 

Tetration is to exponentiation, as exponentiation is to multiplication.

Therefore, $^4 2$ (which looks weird, but that's just the formatting) is:
2^2^2^2 = 2^2^4 = 2^16 = 65536

Why does tetration exist? If I had to make an educated guess, it was a mathematician following the general order of operations:
1. Addition, or a + n
2. Multiplication, or where $a*n$ = $a+a$ n times
3. Exponentiation, or where $a^n$ = $a*a$ n times
4. Tetration, or where $^na$ = $a^a$ n times

What is it useful for? Another educated guess, I would think its for something related to either theoretical mathematics, or science, since tetration quickly becomes extremely large numbers: where you will have to use ~~scientific notation~~ (A quick search on Wikipedia shows that instead most people resort to writing it in base 10 via "iterated exponential notation" since the numbers manage to exceed regular scientific notation very quickly).
