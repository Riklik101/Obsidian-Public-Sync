H2S is a colorless, flammable gas that has a odor similar to rotten eggs. It is heavier than air and will disperse onto the ground; extended exposure leads to olfactory fatigue -- losing the ability to smell the gas. 

## Health
This gas is very dangerous, as the following symptoms occur at each ppm increase:
* 100 ppm
	* Immediately dangerous to heath
* 500 ppm
	* Collapse within 5 min
* 700 ppm
	* 1-2 breaths can cause collapse

The NIOSH recommended limit is 10 ppm. 

## Sources
Hydrogen Sulfide can be found in many sources. Many geothermal areas release H2S. 

Another source of H2S is petroleum refineries - where the hydrodesulfurization process liberates sulfur from petroleum via hydrogen, and the H2S is converted to elemental sulfur via Claus process. 

In Landfills, organic material burial can lead to anaerobic digestion; biogas can be produced. If there is a source of sulfate, bacteria can convert this to H2S. 

## Removal from Water
H2S has two methods usually used to remove from water:
* Continous chlorination
	* Chlorine can be used to react with H2S, resulting in solid sulfur; insoluble.
* Aeration
	* Oxygen is added to water, the reaction between hdyrogen and oxygen result in odorless sulfate.
