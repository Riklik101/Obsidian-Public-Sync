A Pump Station is used to take water from one area and move it to the next. 

Also called lift stations, they use different systems depending on the type. 

### Dry Well
A dry well lift station has two areas - the wet well and the dry well, which has the quipment that transport water, and can be found underground. This makes the wetwall accessable for inspection. 

### Single Wet Well
A single submersible wet well is much more popular, having a singular well with a pump, being much more affordable. All components are adjacent or inside the well itself. 

This wet well is significantly more common. The dangers of a pump station is that it can contain gases such as methane or [[Hydrogen Sulfide]], which, as [[Aghorn Operating Hydrogen Sulfide Release|one company in Texas found out]], is extremely deadly.
