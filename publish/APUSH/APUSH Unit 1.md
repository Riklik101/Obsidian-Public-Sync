## 1.2 Native American Societies Pre-Contact

### Northeastern Native Americans
 * Dominated by Appalacians
	* Extensive native settlements - Hopewellian period from 200 BCE to 500 CE
		* Temperate climate
		* Good farming conditions
		* Accessible waterways
	* notable groups include the Iroquois and others
#### Food
The Hopewellian period included the cultural shift from hunting and gathering to agricultural systems. 
Corn, squash, beans became the Three Sisters - coined by the Iroquois. Women gathered berries and cultivation, men would hunt and occasionally farm. Rivers would be used for fishing. Abundance of food led to pottery and baskets. 
#### Social
The Three Sisters led to cash crops, being used for trade by plains and west coast NAs. NE residents lived in villages with few hundred residents. This culture also led to mound-building, serving burial and ceremonial purposes. 

This also created the great league of peace
