A basketball hoop standardly has a net. This net is usually made of cloth for indoor areas, while chain is used outside due to it's durability and vandal resistance. 

Chain net comes at a downside that it can scratch the basketball itself.

#### Alternatives to standard nets

The infinet is a netless basketball hoop replacement designed to be an alternative to chain or net. Australian made, it has the goal to being tough, being made of steel and UV stable film, and tamper proof fixings. 

the [spec sheet](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/68356312f068937ff02773b5/t/684100a8802b3b41e6fe0d67/1749090476249/Infinet+fin+kit+spec+sheet.pdf) states that it uses torx 25 fasteners for the fins themselves, specifically tamper proof zinc plated ones. The fins are made of UV resistant polymer with a "reinforced core" (however no information is given regarding that this means and it may just be a marketing term.)

##### Opinions of this infinet by some basketball friends
Personal opinion is that this could be useful however the cost also comes into play; is this more or less expensive then simply a chain or cloth netting being used as this is a proprietary product and therefore is not easily found in othr manufacturing senses

*"Expert Opinion"*
My thoughts are that while it is a cheap and viable substitute for an actual basketball net, it would either not work or be very unpopular for a number of reasons. 
1, I'm not entirely sure on this one, but it looks quite weak, meaning it could be susceptible to breaking in weather or if ppl try to dunk(even if you say no dunking no one ever follow the rule), 
2, the sound of a basketball net or chain is quite iconic, so many people will be put off when they hear a completely different sound, because it's so synonymous with bball. the lack of sound would be a big turn off for most basketball enjoyers. 
3, the net is a very important part of recreational basketball because it is the clearest indicator of if a shot went in or not. 

Without cameras or rule keepers, the game is much easier to understand if you can see a visual indication that a shot went in, which this does not provide. My personal thoughts would be that I would play on it if it's the only thing around, but I definitely would not prefer it over a net or even chain(and I think this is a very popular opinion)
