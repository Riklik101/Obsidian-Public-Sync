* Basic fall protections
	* Fall restraints - cable clipped into an anchor point that is before any ledge that could make you fall
		* Limits the amount of working space
		* cheapest option
		* easiest option too
	* Unpadded fall belt - small falls
		* for edges that lead to small falls and such
		* for accidental oversights
		* not much use but could be useful in some isolated cases
	* ratchet fall protection - for rollercoasters and construction
		* low maintenance - its literally a ratchet
		* loud, as it is a ratcheting clank
		* fail-safe design as each ratchet ensures maximized protection
		* very sudden stop and therefore prone to injury
		* optimal for high tgraffic designs
	* Mechanical friction fall protection - via a motor or some other means
		* friction means rope must be made of nonflammable and high grip
		* friction means that the fall will be slowly stopped
		* requires longer fall distance - not optimal for falls where a sudden stop is needed to protect
		* less durable - friction is very bad for ropes
* Advanced fall protection
	* airbag type system - for walking outside and those who are very clumsy
		* requires computer charging or battery
		* expensive 
		* requires accelerometers and other mechanical parts prone to failure
		* requires fail-safe design
		* very safe and helpful
		* can be designed to be unobtrusive
	* combined mechanical and ratchet protection
		* optimal for wind turbines and other types
		* if a fall occurs, friction would be first used, slowing down the user, as the ratchet kicks in
		* could be a user controlled option - where friction will kick in and if it is a very short fall the ratchet will engage by request
			* it would be a fail safe design - user must press on friction button or switch for friction to be option, otherwise will default to ratcheting
		* weight of user and force of fall could be manipulated to result in the clip frictioning and pressing down onto clipped rope making fall very slow
			* this also means the clip can be moved up only when user is goes up- if there is no force on the clip.

* misc
	* computer controlled fall protection
		* presumably could be used on a bridge or some other type of construction
		* either overhead or from the side a continuous rope is used- moving along a belay that is on bearings 
		* if the person does fall or trip the belay activates and the fall is either slowed or stopped. 
		* computers can determine how much rope is necessary
		* can be useful as a in vitru form of fall restraints - if a worker does not need to be in a certain area, the rope will literally stop them
		* can also help with if a crane or other construction equipment is being used
		* would be expensive for setup and require monitoring
		* not sure about if it is efficient considering other options for protection
	* fall nets
		* either out of rope, nylon (be careful of rope burn), cable, etc
		* catches a person
		* very common to see off of construction of bridges or as suicide nets - in buildings and such
		* tradeoff between durability and safety - cable nets are durable ut will cause injury 
			* important to realize that these are the last resort measure and comfort is of least priority
